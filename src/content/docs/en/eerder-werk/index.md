Before FCD took its current form, I worked with a model of axes, scenarios and showcase practices. I keep that here. It is input for the [backlog](../backlog.md).

- **The model with axes:** [model-assen.html](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd/blob/main/Backlog/model-assen.html) in the repository, and [the picture of the axes](/assen.svg).
- **[Scenarios](../scenarios/index.md):** the model with axes filled in, per scenario.
- **[Showcase practice](../showcase-practice/index.md):** one right practice as working code, such as contract-based testing.
- **[Open items](../open-punten.md):** what was still open for the model with axes.

These pages are in Dutch only and use the terms of that time, such as *deelsysteem* (subsystem) and *fabriek* (factory). In the FCD model these are sub-product and Full Circle Delivery.
