These are the considerations behind FCD. For each one: the question, my consideration and what it means for FCD.

## FCD without AI too

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** does FCD work without AI?

**Consideration:** yes. A user who is senang, the circle, principles with comply or explain, and ownership all existed before AI. But without AI I could not have built FCD-demo. And without 40+ years of experience AI would not have built it well: AI brought the speed, experience the direction. AI amplifies what is there, the good and the bad.

**For FCD:** the principles are experience, captured so that AI can work with it.

## Principles, balance and context

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** do principles slow you down?

**Consideration:** I want fast results. Principles without explain become a straitjacket, explain without principles becomes chaos. Comply or explain keeps speed and principles in balance. And principles must take context into account: people, process and product.

**For FCD:** the principle says what and why, for everyone. The design decision fills it in for your context. The explain is a conscious deviation, visible, with a reason, a human who approves and an end date.

## People, process, product: the chain

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** what is process, and who owns it?

**Consideration:** people, process, product applies at every level. The user has a work process and uses running software for it. The squads own running software, made with FCD. The team that provides platform, CI/CD, observability, SRE and Ops owns running FCD. Not "made with", but "owner of". Process is more than FCD.

**For FCD:** the product of one level carries the process of the next, up to the work process of the user. That is what you do it for. See [the FCD model](model.md#people-process-product).

## The platform: within the tribe

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** does the team for the FCD products belong in the tribe, or to the whole enterprise?

**Consideration:** in the tribe. My ideal organisation is a tribe for a number of personas of a (sub)domain, with squads that have autonomy in the how, and a team that provides platform, CI/CD, observability, SRE and Ops. A platform team for the whole enterprise does not work effectively and efficiently, in my view. Whether that still holds in the AI era, I do not know yet. Not an enabling team: that helps temporarily, this team remains the owner.

**For FCD:** the tribe owns FCD, the team owns the FCD products, the squads own the product.

## Standard or custom

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** buy or build, now that AI can generate software?

**Consideration:** what sets you apart (your core domain) is custom. What everyone needs (commodity) you take as a product. In between, the context decides. AI makes custom software cheap, but not owning it. To me, generate is simply build. 4GL, 5GL and low code also promised faster code, and got stuck on what no generator solves: the intent, the ownership and the lifecycle.

**For FCD:** whatever you choose, someone is the owner and the circle goes round.

## The intent is the source

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** what is the source: the intent or the code?

**Consideration:** the intent, and nothing else. Code is a valuable intermediate step: one captured translation of the intent, which you can read, test, scan, version and roll back.

**For FCD:** the intent is the source, code is an intermediate step, the product for the user is the goal.

## Variation in the making, reliability for the user

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** must AI be deterministic?

**Consideration:** not in the making. People are not deterministic either, and people cannot even write a complete and reliable intent. The same intent does not lead to exactly the same result, and that is fine. Reliability belongs with the user: the guest expects every order to arrive.

**For FCD:** what runs is exactly what was checked (build once, deploy many). Incomplete intent and variation in the making are the reason for the circle: check, measure, learn, update the intent.

## One language for people, AI and machines

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** why do we still have programming languages?

**Consideration:** in the future I see one language that people, AI and machines can read. No .NET, Java or TypeScript. AI translates and explains, so the language does not have to be easy for people, but it must be precise. The intent describes the what and why. The language describes the what and how.

**For FCD:** the what in the intent and the what in the language must match. That is where the contract and the check sit.

## Verify and validate, continuously

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** what does a test actually check?

**Consideration:** "test" carries two meanings. I borrow the well-known terms. Verify: does the product work well? You measure that with KPIs, for the products and the FCD products. Validate: does the product equal the intent? You measure that with the objective and the key results. Both continuously, not at two moments.

**For FCD:** see [the FCD model](model.md#verify-and-validate).

## History instead of a status report

*28 November 2026*

**Question:** in continuous compliance, do you still need a report or an audit?

**Consideration:** the audit as a moment afterwards does not fit continuous compliance: the evidence is created at every step. The status report as a steering tool is a legacy function. Whoever wants to know how things are going looks live. A snapshot at a chosen moment remains valuable, for history and trend analysis.

**For FCD:** the data produces the snapshot, not the other way round. Nobody writes it, and it cannot differ from what really happened.
