## The reason

Everyone wants to develop faster with artificial intelligence (AI). But what good is fast development if you cannot take it to production and change it?

Hence: AI needs delivery. Delivery needs AI. Not either, but both.

## Vision

1. Together we work for the user; the user must be *senang* (at ease, satisfied), now and later.
2. The user gets there by using the product.
3. The product comes from intention and delivery, in balance.
4. What usage teaches us feeds the next intention.
5. The product is never finished.

## The user in the middle

It is about the user. The user must be *senang*: at ease, now and later. The user gets there by using the product.

The business must be at ease too. But the user is central.

## The circle

Around the user and the product turns a circle: intention, delivery, product and learning. What usage teaches us feeds the next intention. You will recognise plan-do-check-act in it.

That is why the product is never finished. And that is why it is called Full Circle Delivery (FCD): not just continuous delivery, but the full circle.

We do this together, and in balance.

## The perspectives

You look at the circle from perspectives: domain, team, agile and DevOps, continuous delivery, testing, quality, architecture and security. And AI.

Each perspective has principles. Together they are the guardrails for AI. The rule of thumb is comply or explain: you follow the principle, or you explain why not.

AI amplifies what is there. Every empty perspective slows effectiveness down. You don't have to wait with AI, but you do need to know which perspective needs attention. The empty box on the picture asks that question: which perspective is missing for you?

## Transparent

The whole is transparent. An objective belongs with the intention. Key performance indicators (KPIs) belong with the product. The dashboard shows how things are going.

## What you get

1. **[The FCD model](model.md).** One language: the building blocks, described and placed per perspective. Business, IT and teams talk about the same thing.
2. **[Your FCD model](jouw-fcd-model.md).** Through questions and answers: where you are, where you want to go and which perspective needs attention. Captured in a declarative file: what and why, reproducible.
3. **[Your FCD model as a working showcase](showcases/index.md).** You see how it can work and where changes are needed.
