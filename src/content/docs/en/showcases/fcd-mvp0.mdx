The first, smallest way of working of Full Circle Delivery that goes the full circle: intention, process, product, learning. AI as accelerator. [ShowKitchen-MVP0](/fcd/en/showcases/showkitchen-mvp0/) was made with it.

## The experiment

Are one intent and one description of a sub-product together enough for an AI squad to build the sub-product?

## What the squad gets

**The intent**, from the persona:

1. Persona and user story
2. Why (objective)
3. Measure (key result)
4. Behaviour as scenarios

Why and measure are separate: the why is the direction and changes little; the measure you adjust after what you learn.

**The description of the sub-product**, from the architecture:

1. Responsibility: what it does, what it does not
2. Users, internal and external
3. Interfaces: what it provides and uses
4. Which intents it fulfils

The intent is not the sub-product. Which sub-product fulfils an intent is an architecture choice.

## Who does the work

The squad is Claude Code with agents: AI agents only. That is why the product owner owns the sub-product and approves deviations. Accountability always lies with a human.
