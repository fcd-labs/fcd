## From fcd to fcd-demo to showcase

It starts with fcd, the FCD model: one language, the FCD language, with the building blocks of your FCD placed per perspective. With it you capture your FCD model in the repo fcd-demo: where you are, where you want to go and which perspective needs attention, in declarative files in the FCD language. And the showcase fcd-demo makes your FCD model work: you see how it can work and where changes are needed.

## Describing fcd-demo

In fcd-demo one product owner and two AI squads work together (people): a squad for sk-bestellen, and a platform squad for the platform, the pipeline and the Dashboard. They work from intent to product (process), on sk-bestellen (product). Within the squad there is autonomy in the how, within the principles: comply or explain.

## The principles in fcd-demo

Every perspective has a principle, but fcd-demo does not show them all. We look at architecture, testing and security; the principles themselves are in the FCD language in fcd-demo.

For architecture, sk-bestellen is custom-built, schema first: a microservice, with the sub-product as deployable unit, and secure. Testing is automated: every test runs in the pipeline. And security means secure software, checked with every change.

Where fcd-demo deviates from a principle, it says so, with an explanation and an approval: comply or explain.

## What you see in fcd-demo

Back to that one order.

| Link | What you see | What you tell |
|---|---|---|
| **User** | A guest orders a steak and a draught beer. The kitchen gets to work; the bar gets nothing. | The user is not at ease. That is where everything starts. |
| **Learning** | The Dashboard: the key result is red at the bar (stub). | We measure what the intent promises. What usage teaches feeds the next intention. |
| **Intention** | The intent Ordering, with its objective and key result. | The intent stays; the code may differ every time. |
| **Delivery** | The patch by the AI squad of sk-bestellen, the Approval by the product owner, the pipeline live with steps, quality gates, deviations and backlog. | People and AI, speed and quality. The evidence arises along the way. |
| **Product** | Ordering 1.0.1: the order reaches the bar, the key result is green. | The guest gets his beer. The product is at ease again, and never finished. |

Transparent throughout the circle: everyone looks at the same Dashboard, with the same evidence.

That is continuous compliance. Not fixing in advance what the code must look like, but showing with every change that the product does what the intent promises, within the principles. The intent stays; the code may differ every time. The evidence arises along the way, and everyone sees the same.

## The perspectives in fcd-demo

Every perspective has a principle: together the guardrails for AI, with comply or explain.

| Perspective | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| Domain | The restaurant, the guest, the intent Ordering |
| Team | Two AI squads, for sk-bestellen and for the platform, with the product owner as owner; autonomy in the how, within the principles |
| Agile/DevOps | A small change, live quickly, and measuring what it does |
| Continuous Delivery | One pipeline, the sub-product as the unit, the only way to production |
| Testing | Automated: unit tests, contract test, integration tests, end-to-end |
| Quality | The quality gates, and the key result as the measure |
| Architecture | Custom-built, schema first (the contract), microservice, the sub-product as deployable unit, secure; frontend and backend, the stubs, one repository per sub-product |
| Security | Secure software: security and freshness, SBOM and licences |
| *Empty* | The question to the viewer: which perspective is missing for you? |
| AI | The AI squad built it; AI amplifies what is there |

## The empty box

At the end you point to the empty perspective. AI amplifies what is there: every empty perspective slows effectiveness. Which perspective is missing for you?

That is the bridge to the conversation, and to your FCD: where you are, where you want to go, and which perspective needs attention. Then the question: what do you think of the model?
