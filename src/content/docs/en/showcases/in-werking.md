## The core - fcd

fcd-demo in action. In the middle the user, who must be at ease (senang), and the product. Around them the circle. Beside them the perspectives, each with a principle. Through it all, transparency. And everywhere: together, in balance, not either-or but and.

The showcase fcd-demo does not tell the whole, it shows it.

One order does not reach the bar. You follow that one order all the way round the circle, and along the way every perspective takes part.

## The circle in fcd-demo

| Link | What you see | What you tell |
|---|---|---|
| **User** | A guest orders a steak and a draught beer. The kitchen gets to work; the bar gets nothing. | The user is not at ease. That is where everything starts. |
| **Learning** | The Dashboard: the key result is red at the bar (stub). | We measure what the intent promises. What usage teaches feeds the next intention. |
| **Intention** | The intent Ordering, with its objective and key result. | The intent stays; the code may differ every time. |
| **Delivery** | The patch by the AI squad, the Approval by the product owner, the pipeline live with steps, quality gates, deviations and backlog. | People and AI, speed and quality. The evidence arises along the way. |
| **Product** | Ordering 1.0.1: the order reaches the bar, the key result is green. | The guest gets his beer. The product is at ease again, and never finished. |

Transparent throughout the circle: everyone looks at the same Dashboard, with the same evidence.

## The perspectives in fcd-demo

Each perspective has a principle: together the guardrails for AI, with comply or explain.

| Perspective | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| Domain | The restaurant, the guest, the intent Ordering |
| Team | A squad of AI agents only, with the product owner as owner |
| Agile/DevOps | A small change, live quickly, and measuring what it does |
| Continuous Delivery | One pipeline, the sub-product as the unit, the only way to production |
| Testing | Unit tests, contract test, integration tests, end-to-end |
| Quality | The quality gates, and the key result as the measure |
| Architecture | Frontend and backend, contract first, the stubs, one repository per sub-product |
| Security | Security and freshness, SBOM and licences |
| *Empty* | The question to the viewer: which perspective is missing for you? |
| AI | The AI squad built it; AI amplifies what is there |

## The empty box

At the end you point to the empty perspective. AI amplifies what is there: every empty perspective slows effectiveness. Which perspective is missing for you?

That is the bridge to the conversation, and to your FCD: where you are, where you want to go, and which perspective needs attention. Then the question: what do you think of the model?
