## The experiment

Are one intent and one description of a sub-product together enough for an AI squad to build the sub-product?

## The intent: Ordering

1. **Persona and user story:** as a guest I want to choose from a menu, so that I can pass my order to the kitchen, bar and till, so that I get my order.
2. **Why (objective):** the guest can order.
3. **Measure (key result):** every order from the guest arrives at kitchen, bar and till.
4. **Behaviour as scenarios:**
   - Given a menu, when the guest chooses, then the order goes to kitchen, bar and till.
   - Given a placed order, then the guest sees that the order was sent to kitchen, bar and till.

Why and measure are separate: the why is the direction and changes little; the measure you adjust after what you learn. What is not described is out of scope.

## The sub-product: Ordering

1. **Responsibility.** Does: show the menu, let the guest choose, and send the order to kitchen, bar and till. Does not: manage the menu, or payment. The menu is a fixed, declarative file in the repository; changing the menu goes through the pipeline.
2. **Users:** the guest.
3. **Interfaces:** Ordering sends one order, the whole order, directly to kitchen, bar and till, according to a contract (contract first).
4. **Intents:** Ordering.

In this version kitchen, bar and till exist as stubs: separate containers, not part of the sub-product. The intent is not the sub-product: which sub-product fulfils an intent is an architecture choice.
