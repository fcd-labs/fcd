**AI needs delivery. Delivery needs AI.**

Everyone wants to develop faster with artificial intelligence (AI). But what good is fast development if you cannot take it to production and change it?

Not either, but both.

[![Talking picture Full Circle Delivery (FCD) 0.8.10](/praatplaat-fcd.png)](/praatplaat-fcd.png "Click for the talking picture at full size")

*Click the picture for full size. The picture is in Dutch.*

## Vision

1. Together we work for the user; the user must be *senang* (at ease, satisfied), now and later.
2. The user gets there by using the product.
3. The product comes from intention and process, in balance.
4. What usage teaches us feeds the next intention.
5. The product is never finished.

## The circle

Intention → process → product → learning → intention again. In the middle the user, at ease through the product.

Not just continuous delivery, but the full circle. Together and in balance.

Transparent: an objective with the intention, key performance indicators (KPIs) with the product, a dashboard in the middle.

## The perspectives

Domain, team, agile and DevOps, continuous delivery, testing, quality, architecture, security and AI. Plus an empty perspective: which perspective is missing for you?

Each perspective has principles: the guardrails for AI. Comply or explain.

AI amplifies what is there. Every empty perspective slows effectiveness down. Don't wait with AI, but know which perspective needs attention.

## What you get

1. **The FCD model.** One language: the building blocks of your FCD, described and placed per perspective. Business, IT and teams talk about the same thing. With two examples: classic and DevOps.
2. **Your FCD model.** Through questions and answers: where you are, where you want to go and which perspective needs attention. Captured in a declarative file: what and why, reproducible.
3. **Your FCD model as a working showcase.** You see how it can work and where changes are needed.
