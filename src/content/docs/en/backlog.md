What the FCD model asks for and is not yet filled in is not a gap. It is backlog: work still to come. Grow through doing.

## Where the backlog is

- **Issues:** the work on FCD is tracked as issues in [fcd-labs/fcd](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd/issues). The fcd board will follow.
- **Showcases:** each picture shows the backlog of that showcase. In [Demo](showcases/demo.md) there are now 5 on the product picture and 4 on the Full Circle Delivery picture. [Classic](showcases/classic.md) and [Ideal](showcases/ideal.md) are still placeholders.
- **[Your FCD](/fcd/en/jouw-fcd/):** the questions per perspective.
- **The model:** the objects are an inventory, far from complete. See [the FCD model](model.md).

## Open in the model

- How do library and open source software attach to the container?
